MMI 1 (Not Felt): Not felt except by a very few people under especially favorable conditions. Tremors detected only by sensitive seismographs. No damage to structures.
MMI 2 (Weak): Felt only by a few persons at rest, especially on upper floors of buildings. Delicately suspended objects may swing slightly. No structural or content damage.
MMI 3 (Weak): Felt noticeably indoors, especially on upper floors of buildings, but many people do not recognize it as an earthquake. Hanging objects swing. Standing motor cars may rock slightly. Vibrations like passing of a light truck.
MMI 4 (Light): Felt indoors by many, outdoors by few during the day. At night, some awakened. Dishes, windows, doors disturbed; walls make cracking sounds. Sensation like heavy truck striking building. Standing vehicles rocked noticeably.
MMI 5 (Moderate): Felt by nearly everyone; many awakened. Some dishes, glassware, and windows broken; unanchored objects overturned. Plaster may crack or fall. Pendulum clocks may stop. Small trees and poles swayed.
MMI 6 (Strong): Felt by all, many frightened and run outdoors. Some heavy furniture moved or overturned; a few instances of fallen plaster or damaged chimneys. Damage slight to moderate in poorly constructed buildings.
MMI 7 (Very Strong): Difficult to stand. Noticed by drivers of motor cars. Damage negligible in buildings of good design and construction; slight to moderate in well-built ordinary structures; considerable damage in poorly built or badly designed structures. Some chimneys broken, plaster fallen, waves on ponds.
MMI 8 (Severe): Damage slight in specially designed structures; considerable in ordinary substantial buildings with partial collapse; great in poorly built structures. Panel walls thrown out of frame structures. Fall of chimneys, factory stacks, monuments, columns, and walls. Heavy furniture overturned. Sand and mud ejected in small amounts.
MMI 9 (Violent): General panic. Damage considerable in specially designed structures, well-designed frame structures thrown out of plumb. Damage great in substantial buildings, with partial or complete collapse. Buildings shifted off foundations. Ground cracked conspicuously. Underground pipes broken, serious damage to reservoirs.
MMI 10 (Very Violent / Extreme): Some well-built wooden structures destroyed; most masonry and frame structures destroyed with foundations. Ground badly cracked and fissured. Rails bent. Landslides considerable along river banks and steep slopes. Shifted sand and mud. Water splashed over riverbanks and canals.
